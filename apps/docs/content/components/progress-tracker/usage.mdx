## Usage

Use a progress tracker for tasks that span several pages, such as setup or checkout.

## Best practices

- Use short stage labels of one or two words.
- Let people go back to visited stages with `href`; stages ahead stay plain text.
- Keep the tracker at the top of every page of the task.
