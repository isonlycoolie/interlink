## Usage

Modal dialogs and drawers bring their own blanket. Use Blanket directly only for custom layers that need to dim or
block the page.

## Best practices

- Tint the blanket when the layer needs people's full attention.
- Let a click on the blanket close the layer, unless closing would lose work.
