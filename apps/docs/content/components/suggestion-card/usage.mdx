---
title: Suggestion card usage
description: A recommendation from an agent that people accept, change or dismiss.
---

## Usage

A suggestion card proposes one action. Nothing changes until people accept it, so agents can help without
surprising anyone.

## Best practices

- Write the title as the action that will happen.
- Explain why in one sentence and link the pages or items it is based on.
- After accepting, replace the buttons with what happened.
