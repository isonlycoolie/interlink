## Usage

Build a conversation from its parts: `ChatPanel` holds a `ChatHeader`, a `ChatThread` of `ChatMessage` items and a
`ChatFooter` with the prompt. Dock the panel beside the work, or float it as a card.

## Best practices

- Greet people in the empty thread and offer two or three prompt suggestions.
- Show the agent's name and hexagon on its answers; people's messages need neither.
- Keep the AI disclaimer under the prompt, where it is seen but quiet.

## Accessibility

- The thread is a log, so new messages are announced without moving focus.
