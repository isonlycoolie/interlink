## Usage

A page header starts every full page. Breadcrumbs say where the page sits, the title says what it is, and the
actions say what people can do with the whole page. A bottom bar holds tabs, search and filters that act on
everything below.

## Content

- Keep the title short and specific, such as "Payments board", not "Board for the payments team".
- Put the primary action last, and no more than three actions before a more menu.
