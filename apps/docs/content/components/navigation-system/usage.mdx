## Usage

The navigation system gives every page of an app the same frame, so people always know which app they are in, where
they are inside it, and how to get somewhere else.

## Parts

- **Top bar** (48px): sidebar toggle, app switcher and the app logo at the start; search and the create button in the
  middle; notifications, help and the person's avatar at the end.
- **Side navigation** (240px): the space people are in at the top, then groups of 32px rows. The current page has a
  selected fill and a bar on its leading edge. Trees of pages open with `ExpandableNavItem`.
- **Main**: the page, starting with a page header. The skip link lands here.
- **Panel**: detail or a short task beside the page, opposite the side navigation.

Below 48rem the side navigation and panel open over the page instead of beside it.

## Best practices

- Put the app logo in the same place on every page and link it home.
- Keep one create button, in the top bar, for the thing people make most.
- Name sections in the side navigation only when there are more than one.
