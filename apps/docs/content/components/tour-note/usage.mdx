## Usage

Use tour notes the first time someone opens a page, to point at the few controls that make it useful. Wrap the control in `TourNote` and the note sits beside it, so nothing it explains is ever covered.

Use a spotlight instead when the step needs a headline, an image or its own buttons.

## Best practices

- Keep each note to one short sentence that says what the control does for people.
- Show two or three notes at most on a page, each on a different control.
- Hide the notes once people have used the control, or give them a way to turn tips off.
- Don't use tour notes for errors, warnings or anything people must act on.
