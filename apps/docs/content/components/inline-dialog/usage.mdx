---
title: Inline dialog usage
description: An inline dialog shows extra detail or a quick task next to its trigger.
---

## Usage

Use an inline dialog for a short explanation or a small task that belongs to one spot on the page. For a task that
needs people's full attention, use a modal dialog.

## Best practices

- Keep content short: a sentence or two, or a couple of fields.
- Open it from a clear trigger, such as a button or a help link.
- Don't put critical information in it; it is easy to close and miss.
