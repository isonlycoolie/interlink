title: Section message usage
description: A section message tells people about something that affects one section of the page.
---

## Usage

Section messages sit inside the content they are about. Use them for information people need while they work in
that section; use a flag for short confirmations and a banner for messages about the whole product.

## Best practices

- Choose the appearance by meaning: information, warning, error, success or discovery.
- Write a short title that summarises the message, then say what happened and what to do next.
- Offer at most two actions, and make them specific.
- Let people dismiss messages that are only informative.

## Accessibility

- Set `headingLevel` so the title fits the page outline.
- Do not rely on color: the title and icon must say what kind of message it is.
