## Usage

Comments collect discussion on a piece of work. Each one shows its author, time and actions, and replies nest under
the comment they answer.

## Best practices

- Keep actions short: Reply, Like, Edit, Delete.
- Show `CommentEdited` when a comment changed after posting.
- Use `highlighted` when people follow a link to a comment.
