## Usage

Use DatePicker for dates, TimePicker for times and DateTimePicker for both. People can pick from the calendar or
list, or type a value.

## Best practices

- Always use a visible label; use `placeholder` only to show the format.
- Limit choices with `minDate`, `maxDate` and `disabled` instead of rejecting values after the fact.
- Turn on `timeIsEditable` when times outside the list are valid.
