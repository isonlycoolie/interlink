title: Generative border usage
description: A four-colour border that marks an AI surface and turns while content is generated.
---

## Usage

The generative border frames a surface where AI is writing. It turns while content is being generated and rests
when it is done, so people can tell at a glance whether the answer is still changing.

## Best practices

- Wrap one surface at a time, usually the prompt or the answer being written.
- Set `isGenerating` only while content is actually changing.
- The motion stops for people who ask for reduced motion; the colours stay.
