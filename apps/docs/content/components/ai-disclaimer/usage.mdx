## Usage

Put the disclaimer under AI answers or the prompt. It reminds people to check the result and gives them a quick way
to say whether it helped.

## Best practices

- Keep the default text unless your product has a specific risk to name.
- Use the rating to improve answers; do not ask for it twice in the same answer.
