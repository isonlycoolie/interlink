---
title: Banner usage
description: A banner is a full-width message at the top of the page about something that affects the whole product.
---

## Usage

Use a banner for product-wide news, such as planned downtime or an expiring trial. For messages about part of a
page, use a section message; for events, use a flag.

## Best practices

- Show one banner at a time, at the very top of the page.
- Keep it to one short line. Link to details instead of explaining in the banner.
- Use the error appearance only when something is broken now.
