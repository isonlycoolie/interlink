---
title: Flag usage
description: A flag is a short notification at the bottom of the screen about an event or action.
---

## Usage

Flags confirm that something happened or report a problem that is not tied to one place on the page. Raise them
with `useFlags()` inside a `FlagsProvider`, or render a `FlagGroup` yourself.

## Best practices

- Write the title as what happened: "Page saved", not "Success".
- Use bold appearances for system messages; use normal flags for confirmations with actions.
- Auto-dismiss only success messages that need no action.
- Offer at most two short actions, such as Undo or View.

## Accessibility

- Warnings and errors are announced straight away; other flags are announced politely.
- Auto-dismissing flags pause while hovered or focused.
