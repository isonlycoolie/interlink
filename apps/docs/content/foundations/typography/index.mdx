Type carries most of what a product says. Interlink typography favours clarity over personality: one family for
the interface, one for code, and a short scale where every step has a job.

## Typographic principles

### Optimize for readability

Body text is set at 14px with a 20px line height, sized for long sessions on dense screens. Keep lines between 50
and 90 characters and give paragraphs room.

### Create visual harmony

Heading, body and metric styles share one family and a common grid, so text lines up with icons, controls and the
8px spacing scale.

## Text styles and tokens

Each style is a single composite token that sets size, line height, weight and family together. Apply it with
the `Heading`, `Text` and `MetricText` primitives or with `font` in xcss, never with separate raw properties.
